Kerr ISCO: a factor-of-two error found and fixed

The original Schwarzschild ISCO implementation used 12GM/c² instead of 6GM/c². The exact Bardeen–Press–Teukolsky solution now matches the Schwarzschild limit and reproduces prograde and retrograde branches across spin.

Energetics

How much rotational energy survives a merger?

Across 270 remnants with complete masses and effective spin, the median inferred spin is 0.69 and the thin-disc efficiency 0.10 — far below the Kerr bound of 0.42. A Schwarzschild ISCO overestimates the true radius by 1.76× on average.
