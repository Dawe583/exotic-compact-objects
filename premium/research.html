What we are testing

Concrete, falsifiable questions rather than a survey of everything.

Q1

Do the remnant observables leave room for exotic alternatives?

Compare the Kerr ISCO, ringdown frequency and horizon area of observed remnants with the predictions of horizonless and wormhole models.

Q2

How much does spin change the radiative efficiency?

Map the ISCO binding energy across the prograde and retrograde branches and propagate it into the remnant energy budget.

Q3

Can early supermassive black holes grow within the Eddington limit?

Confront the reverberation-mapped mass function and the high-redshift seed models with the observed growth budget.

Q4

Which stability tests actually discriminate?

Raychaudhuri focusing, white-hole horizon instabilities and wormhole flare-out conditions compared on the same footing.
